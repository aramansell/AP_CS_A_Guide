RPG Project Files

Throughout the year, you build an RPG piece by piece. Each phase adds files to your project. By May, everything connects: your Dice engine rolls damage, Combat resolves fights, Player and Monster classes hold state, files load the bestiary and maps, and after the exam, the GameCharacter hierarchy unifies it all into one engine.
