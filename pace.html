Pacing Calendar, 2026-27

Every A-day of the school year, mapped to its work. Built from the PPS district calendar (breaks, planning days, holidays) and our A/B alternation. The class meets on A days only; this calendar is the contract between the syllabus and the clock.

The shape: four phases (the four CED units), a two-week sprint, the exam on May 12, 2027 at 12:00 PM (Session 2), then Season 2: inheritance and the engine room, post-exam, for the love of the game. All 53 required CED topics are covered by April 26. The last two weeks are pure sharpening.
