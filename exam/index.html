Exam Hub

The AP Computer Science A exam is Wednesday, May 12, 2027, 12:00 PM (Session 2). Three hours. Everything you need to get ready lives here. This page is your base camp from April 28 (the first sprint day) to the finish line.

The format, in one glance: 42 multiple-choice questions, 90 minutes, 55% of your score. Then 4 free-response questions, 90 minutes, 45%. You get the Java Quick Reference sheet in the room. Four answer choices per MC question (down from five in the old days, every wrong answer is now a plausible lie).

How to Use This Hub

  1. All year: keep the JQR printed at your workstation. Every "which method?" question in a lab is a JQR lookup question. Train the reflex now, not in May.
  2. From January: after each FRQ day (E1, E2, E3, E4), read the matching FRQ Center page and self-score with the rubric. Rubric eyes catch points that "I think I got it" eyes miss.
  3. From April 28: this hub is home. Mock, score, list your errors by topic, drill your weakest two topics, repeat. The clinics exist to answer your specific error list. Bring it.
