Mock Exam Protocol

April 28 (multiple choice) and April 30 (free response) are full simulations: real timing, real formats, real scoring. The mock's purpose is not the score. It is the error list. Every point you miss becomes a line on a personal diagnosis that the May 4 and May 6 clinics treat directly. A mock without a diagnosis is just a bad Tuesday.

April 28: Section I Simulation

TimeWhat happens
0:00-0:05Logistics. Phones away, JQR on desk, booklet distributed. Same rules as May 12.
0:05-1:3542 questions, 90 minutes, no interruptions. Practice the timing plan from the strategy page: first pass, mark, second pass, sweep.
1:35-1:50Self-score with the key. Then the diagnosis sheet: for each miss, write the archetype (trace / error / complete / call / count / responsibility) and the root cause (misread, trap, rushed, unknown topic).

April 30: Section II Simulation

TimeWhat happens
0:00-0:05Format briefing. Four booklets: Q1 methods, Q2 class, Q3 ArrayList, Q4 2D array.
0:05-1:3590 minutes, 25 points. Follow the FRQ time plan. The provided Round B questions are the mock material.
1:35-1:50Pause. Rubrics are distributed May 4, not today. Sit with the uncertainty; that too is exam-realistic.

May 4: Rubric Workshop

Trade booklets. Score a classmate's FRQs with the real rubrics, row by row. Scoring someone else's work does three things a self-score cannot:

  1. It forces rubric-language into your head ("calls the method" vs "re-implements it").
  2. It shows you points you would have missed claiming ("I got that one" is often wrong in both directions).
  3. It is exactly how AP readers are trained. You are doing the real job, on real work.

Then the MC error clinic: your diagnosis sheet, sorted by root cause. Rushed misses get pacing drills. Trap misses get the decoy list. Unknown topics get the docs page and a 20-minute focused re-do. Every person's clinic is different because every error list is different.

May 6: JQR Speed Drills + Weak-Topic Clinic

Timed retrieval rounds: signature to English, English to signature, find the method, write the loop. Then your last two weak topics, chosen from the May 4 data, get 40 minutes each of focused work with the teacher circulating. This is the last structured day before the exam. End it with a plan for the weekend, not with panic: two 45-minute sessions Saturday and Sunday, on your list, then rest.

Root causes, pick from these five, in priority order:

CauseTreatment
Unknown topicdocs page + lesson re-do + 3 practice problems. Biggest time investment, biggest payoff.
Trap (knew it, decoy got me)Decoy list, name the exact misconception. 10 minutes.
Misread the questionUnderline practice: subject, verb, condition, on every question stem. 20 minutes.
Rushed / pacingTwo-minute timer drills, ten questions.
Traced in headTable discipline: every trace on paper, no exceptions, five questions.

Sort by frequency, treat top two. That is the clinic. That is the last two weeks. It works because it is aimed.
